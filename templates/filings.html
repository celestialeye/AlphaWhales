Record semantics

Published means the accession was selected and durably written during that processing run; it is a historical outcome, not proof that it remains the current source. Recorded means the filing was retained but not selected. Historical identifies accession metadata copied from the local SEC archive. Baselined identifies the recent inventory captured when daily operations were first deployed.
