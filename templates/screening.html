How to read this screen

Reported size ≠ AUM

13F value excludes cash, shorts, private assets, most bonds, and many derivatives.

Turnover is estimated

Routine operations are removed using share and weight deadbands; complete price-drift adjustment remains pending.

Duration is observed

Repeated quarter-end appearances do not prove uninterrupted ownership between filings.

Performance is hypothetical

The current-cohort sleeve trades after public disclosure and is shown before fees, taxes, and transaction costs—not as actual fund performance.
